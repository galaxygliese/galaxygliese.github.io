I'm **Sodtavilan Odonchimed (Sodoo)**, a machine learning researcher and engineer working on
generative AI for vision and robotics. I hold an M.Eng. from the University of Tokyo,
where I did research at the Matsuo-Iwasawa Lab on diffusion models for images and
robot learning. I am currently interested in applying to a PhD program.

## Research areas

::: {.interests}
- Diffusion models, imitation learning, and behavior cloning for robot learning
- Computer vision, image generation, and medical image synthesis
- Physics-aware generative models and signal processing
:::
